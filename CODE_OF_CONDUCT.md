# Code of Conduct

CancelLens welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. These rules apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code and evidence respectfully; critique proposals rather than people.
- Explain technical disagreements and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and differing experiences.
- Keep collaboration focused and help others participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, and publication of private information without consent are unacceptable.
Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in a review is
also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [the maintainer](mailto:zheltov_pe@mail.ru)
with the subject `[CancelLens conduct]`. Include relevant links or context and your
preferred way to be contacted. Do not post private information publicly. For a concern
involving the maintainer, you can also use GitHub's reporting tools to report abusive
content or an account to GitHub.

The maintainer reviews concerns in context and may request a behavior change, remove
content, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant for serious or
repeated violations. Information from reports is shared only as needed to assess
and address the concern. Maintainers are subject to the same expectations.
